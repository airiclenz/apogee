Problem: ADR 0105 names a seat, but no module owns one. The resolver, appliedGen and the retired flag are copied field by field at every spawn site. The Sub-agents seat's "move once, announce once, re-latch later spawns" is rebuilt in Driver code, twice.

Solution: the seat becomes the engine's unit. A child joins a seat and inherits all of it, and a routed spawn dials the seat's current binding. The four hand-rolled resolver constructors collapse into one, keyed by seat kind, with two option-source adapters (TUI live, Firing fixed).

  • Locality: six bug tickets in one week land in one module
  • Delete the latch slot, BindDelegationLatch, Announce
  • Leverage: bench and daemon re-latch for free
  • Seat tests prove re-latch and announce-once directly

Evidence: background-workflow-no-fallback (backgroundHost copied the holder and appliedGen, not the resolver), firing-routed-relatch-gap, fallback-lander-stale-options, routed-rehome-root-pins, 715c380e (the namer dialled the dead primary).

Reopens the writer's call in fallback-followups plan 01 ("Firing re-latch: Driver-side"). This is not an owner ruling. It keeps ADR 0105 D8, since the resolver is still injected per seat.

1 · The seat owns its fallover

It is the hottest area: six follow-up bugs in the week the feature shipped, each one a Driver or spawn site forgetting a step the seat should own.
