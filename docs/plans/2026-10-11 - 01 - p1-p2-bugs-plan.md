## 9. Console-output codepage decoder in internal/platform

**What:** Recast at the regression check (2026-10-11).
**Goal:**
- internal/platform exports a pure, untagged `DecodeConsoleOutput(b []byte, codepage uint32) string`.
- It decides per line, not per buffer: each line that is valid UTF-8 passes untouched, and each invalid line is decoded through a codepage→`golang.org/x/text` encoding table.
