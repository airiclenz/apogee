A handler that edits without setting it is still booked; one that sets it without editing is booked as `intercept` too.
- *writer* **Retry stops its leg, budget-blind (round three):** at `post-response` the first `Retry` stops the leg it fires in — builtins, then armed — whatever the remaining retry budget; the budget is a LOOP concern and only decides whether the loop re-streams (`loop.go:459,471`). A builtin `Retry` does NOT suppress the armed leg when the budget is spent (today's `loop.go:459→465` fall-through runs the hooks on the untouched response); it does when budget remains (the loop `continue`s before the hooks run). The budget reaches `fire` through the payload, not the signature — greenfield §9.2's `fire(ctx, m, payload)` stays: the post-response payload is `domain.PostResponseMoment{Resp *Response; Retryable bool}` with `Revision()` forwarding to `Resp`, the same wrapper shape as `ToolResultMoment` (item 3); `PostResponseFunc` handlers still take `*Response`.
