Models see nothing numeric about their context fill; a delegate cannot stop and report before the fold takes its conversation. Ship context-fill-notice: an engine-origin advise Reaction on post-tool-result that appends a fenced trailer at 50/75/90 percent of the compaction line (History allocation), with tokens used and the window, plus a wrap-up sentence for a child at 90. Off by default behind the top-level boolean context-fill-notice; off under Bypass; silent when no window is known. Builds ADR 0076 D6's advise slot (provenance fence, ledger, strip on resume), which stage 3 (apogee-rxj) reuses.
