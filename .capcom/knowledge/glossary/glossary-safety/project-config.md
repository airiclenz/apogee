---
title: "Project config: the Project root's own committable `.apogee/` settings"
---

**Project config**:
The **Project root**'s own `.apogee/` settings, committable with the repo. apogee alone writes it, and only
on the user's answer; tools may not. An entry in it that grants anything — an **Allow rule**, an
`mcp-servers:` entry or a `reactions:` entry (the **Key class** Granting) — is inert until
**Adopted**, and an adopted repo server or reaction runs under the tighter rules **Adoption** names.
_Avoid_: "repo config", "local config", "project settings file".
