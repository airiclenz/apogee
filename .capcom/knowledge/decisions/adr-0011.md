---
title: The TUI is a thin event-driven renderer over a worker-goroutine engine
---

Status: accepted; C2's "deadlock is structurally impossible" narrowed to worker-goroutine emits by its 2026-10-19 Note

> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

The TUI is a thin event-driven renderer: one worker goroutine at a time drives the Agent through
`Step`, and the Bubble Tea `Update` goroutine reaches it only through `p.Send` and a reply channel.
Read `adr-0011-context`, `adr-0011-decision`, `adr-0011-consequences`, then the realisations
`adr-0011-phase-2`, `adr-0011-phase-2b` and `adr-0011-post-v1`, the last naming the few synchronous
engine calls `Update` may make.
