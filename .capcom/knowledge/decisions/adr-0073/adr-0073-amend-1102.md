---
title: "ADR 0073 amendment of 2026-11-02: one Reaction host per Driver root"
---

Ratified by the owner on 2026-11-02 for plan
`2026-11-02 - 07 - observe-reaction-host-plan` (review card #5 of
`docs/reviews/architecture-review-2026-11-02.html`). Decision 9 stands as written; this amendment
widens what its "one library, every root" covers. Until now the library was the Runner and the
Driver-side observe lane around it was built by each root in its own code. From this amendment the
whole observe lane is one library module, the **Reaction host** (`reactions.Host` in
`internal/reactions`, beside the Runner and `DefaultExecutor`):

- **The MCP disarm and its notices.** A root that dials no MCP server (`HostMCPNone`: every
  Firing) disarms the list's `mcp:` entries before arming, one notice per entry
  (`adr-0076-amend-1029`). `apogee probe context` settles its `mcp:` entries through the same
  `reactions.DisarmUnconnectedMCP`, without a host. A root that dials its MCP set later (`HostMCPLate`: the TUI
  session) resolves through a late bind, refusing as "not connected yet" until the set is bound.
- **The url-safety guard and the fenced `DefaultExecutor`.** Every webhook posts through the guard
  over the same allow and deny lists the network tools use, and a global `run:` program inside the
  workspace is refused by the exec fence (decision 6).
- **The late binds.** The project-run runner the Agent binds once it is built, and the MCP resolver.
- **One close grace.** `reactions.CloseGrace` bounds both a root's Runner close and the drain of a
  generation retired by a reload (decision 7), replacing the two equal constants a comment kept in
  step.

**Each Driver root builds its own host**, one per Firing and one per TUI session, and composes its
`domain.Config` through the host's `Install`. The observe lane stays Driver-side (ADR 0076 A8);
nothing here moves it into the engine. `Install` fills `Events`, `ObserveRunner`, `Observe` and
`BindProjectRun`, plus `Report` and `PinMoved` when the options give them. The Driver keeps two
things: its own sink wrapping, laid over `Config.Events` after `Install`, and `Config.MCP`, which
`Install` never sets, because the engine reads a nil resolver as a Driver that dials no server and
an unbound one as a connection not made yet.

**The facade re-exports the host** as `apogee.NewReactionHost`, with the `ReactionHost`,
`ReactionHostOptions` and `ReactionHostMCP` aliases, so an embedder arms the observe lane with the
same fence, guard and one notice per entry as the binary rather than rebuilding them.
`NewReactionRunner` stays for an embedder that brings its own executor.

With this, the rule that nothing safety-relevant may exist in one Driver's surface alone
(`adr-0073-context`, glossary **Driver**) is met for the observe lane: no in-binary Driver arms it
without the host, and a facade Driver can reach the same host.
