---
title: Pointer shapes on hover are OSC 22, probe-gated and off by default
---

Status: accepted

While the mouse hovers, the TUI may set the terminal's pointer shape to what a click there does:
`text` over editable text and drag-selectable transcript lines, `pointer` over clickable rows and
toggleable blocks, `default` elsewhere. It writes the shape as an OSC 22 escape. The feature runs
only when `ui.pointer-shapes` is on (default off) and the terminal has answered an in-session
capability query in full. Otherwise apogee writes no OSC 22 byte and keeps cell-motion mouse
tracking. It is presentational only: no Event, nothing model-visible. Read `adr-0106-context`,
`adr-0106-decision`, `adr-0106-rejected`, `adr-0106-consequences`.
