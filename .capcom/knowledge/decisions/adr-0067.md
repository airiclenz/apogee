---
title: "`system-prompt-layers` are an explicit additive channel"
---

Status: accepted
Amends: ADR 0023 (decision 1's key count)

> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

`system-prompt-layers:` is a top-level list of `text:` or `file:` fragments appended, in listed
order and joined by a blank line, after whatever prompt the ladder selected; layers are not a fifth
rung and never trigger or suppress the embedded default. Read `adr-0067-context`,
`adr-0067-decision`, `adr-0067-rejected`, then `adr-0067-consequences`.
