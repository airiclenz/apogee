Status: accepted; the "cancellation rolls the whole Turn back" consequence superseded by ADR 0088

> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

A Turn is one primary Upstream call and the work after it, an Exchange one user input through to the
final no-tool response; `Step()` advances one Turn and returns at a quiescent boundary, the only
point where snapshot, resume and fork are valid, where a cancel lands and where an extension's panic
is recovered. The whole-Turn rollback on cancel gave way to ADR 0088. Read `adr-0007-context`,
`adr-0007-decision`, `adr-0007-consequences`, then `adr-0007-phase-1`.
