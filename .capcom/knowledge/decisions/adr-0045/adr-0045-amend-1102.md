---
title: "ADR 0045 amendment of 2026-11-02: a fallen-over Sub-agents seat latches its fallback"
---

Decided with ADR 0105 (docs/plans/2026-11-02 - 00 - midrun-model-fallback-plan.md, items 1 and 11). It amends decision 4 for a Sub-agents seat that has fallen over.

The Sub-agent server's entry may carry its own `fallback:` list, and a routed child follows that list, not the session entry's. Once the seat has fallen over, the Delegation target's beats watch the **fallback** entry, and they never latch the dead primary again for the rest of the session. The fallover re-points the routing at the fallback: the monitor's server and the target's name, keeping the configured name beside them. It also pushes the fallback's target into the latch at once, under the same lock a retarget takes, so a routed child spawned before the next beat dials the fallback. A `servers:` save that leaves the key unchanged keeps the fallback. A human door (`/sub-agents-server`, or an edit that moves the key) installs a fresh resolver, so that seat graces its primary again.

> **Amended 2026-11-02 (docs/plans/2026-11-02 - 04 - seat-and-resolved-entry-plan.md, item 13; `adr-0105-amend-1102d`).** The sentence "It also pushes the fallback's target into the latch at once, under the same lock a retarget takes, so a routed child spawned before the next beat dials the fallback" is superseded: no Driver pushes the target any more. The Driver resolves the fallback's target from the beat that found it answering and hands it to the engine on the resolver's answer (`FallbackAnswer.Target`); the engine re-latches the tree from it when the resolver's landing commits, under the latch's own lock, once per landing and only while the latch is still armed on that seat's resolver. The at-once timing holds on every Driver: a routed child spawned before the next beat dials the fallback.

What stands: "every unusable beat still pushes nil into the latch" holds verbatim, now for the fallback entry's beats. An unusable fallback therefore sends spawns to the session's Upstream with the parent's posture, and their results carry `SeatFallbackNote`, as this decision says. The two fallbacks differ. This decision picks a seat at spawn, per spawn, and lands on the session's server. ADR 0105 moves a seat mid-Turn onto an entry its config names. The fallover calls no `SetDelegationSeat` (ADR 0069), and the notice debounce of the 2026-09-02 amendment is unchanged.
