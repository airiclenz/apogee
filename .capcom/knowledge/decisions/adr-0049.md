title: An approved write escape executes through a permit pinned to the disclosed target
---

Status: accepted

An approved out-of-workspace write actually writes: the Gate's allow mints a context permit pinned
to exactly the resolved target the approval pane disclosed, and the whole workspace-write family
honours it. Approval is the bound, with no hard-deny set above the Gate. Read `adr-0049-context`,
`adr-0049-decision`, `adr-0049-consequences`, `adr-0049-amend-0814`, then `adr-0049-amend-0828` and
`adr-0049-note-0914`.
