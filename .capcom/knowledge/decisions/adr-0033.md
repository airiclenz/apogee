title: The scheduler is a library and the TUI is its first Driver surface
---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-02): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.
>
> Note (2026-10-07): the `apogee headless` runner and the daemon this ADR calls deferred both ship:
> `apogee headless` over `internal/run` (`cmd/apogee/headless.go`,
> ADR 0075) and `apogee daemon`,
> which holds the durable schedules (`cmd/apogee/daemon.go`,
> ADR 0034).
> Read "deferred" and "the future daemon" as of 2026-08-03.

The scheduler is a library component and the TUI its first Driver surface: a Schedule runs in Plan
or Auto only under a fail-safe denying Approver, an overlapping tick is skipped, and each Firing
gets fresh context. The headless runner and the daemon it deferred have since shipped (ADR 0075, ADR
0034). Read `adr-0033-context`, `adr-0033-decision`, `adr-0033-rejected`, `adr-0033-consequences`,
then `adr-0033-addendum-0804`.
