Skill suggestion is a Driver concern painted over an engine-level BM25 matcher,
`skills.Catalog.Suggest`, in a band above the input box; nothing about the catalog reaches the
model, and a skill shown at send is spent for the session. ADR 0065's `load_skill` superseded
decision 4's B2 deferral, and the band re-ranks once per pause. Read `adr-0061-context`,
`adr-0061-decision`, `adr-0061-consequences`, `adr-0061-amend-0831`, then `adr-0061-amend-0923`.
