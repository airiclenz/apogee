---
title: apogee remembers the model choice per server
---

Status: accepted; decision 1's OFF default superseded by its own 2026-09-16 amendment (default ON)

apogee records an explicit model choice on the `servers:` entry it was made on (`model:` on a plain
entry, `launch-profile:` on a launcher-fronted one) under one `remember-model` toggle, and restores
the profile at the next TUI start-up through the actuation latch, yielding to any running instance.
Its 2026-09-16 amendment turned the default on. Read `adr-0048-context`, `adr-0048-decision`,
`adr-0048-rejected`, `adr-0048-consequences`, then `adr-0048-amend-0916`.
