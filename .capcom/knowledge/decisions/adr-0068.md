---
title: Unnamed delegations are named out of band, on the child's own Upstream
---

Status: accepted
Amends: ADR 0022 (addendum — the naming call's "emits no events" claim gains a sibling that emits one)

> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

A delegation whose call named nothing gets a short generated name from one out-of-band completion on
the child's own Upstream, through a host-implemented `domain.DelegationNamer`; it runs beside the
child, is gated by `auto-title`, emits one event, replaces the shown name everywhere and fails
silently. Read `adr-0068-context`, `adr-0068-decision`, `adr-0068-rejected`, then
`adr-0068-consequences`.
