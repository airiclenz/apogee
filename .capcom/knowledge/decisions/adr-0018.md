---
title: "Context overflow recovers structurally: the emergency fold and one retry"
---

Status: accepted; a fold fires the `compaction-finished` notice by `adr-0018-amend-1021`

> Note (2026-10-02): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

A wire context overflow recovers structurally, also under Bypass: one emergency fold per Turn,
mid-Exchange if need be, then one retry; a give-up reads as before. Read `adr-0018-context`,
`adr-0018-decision`, `adr-0018-consequences`, `adr-0018-note-0727`, `adr-0018-amend-0802`,
`adr-0018-amend-0802b`, `adr-0018-amend-0826`, `adr-0018-addendum-0922`, then `adr-0018-amend-1003` and `adr-0018-amend-1021`.
