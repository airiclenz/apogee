title: "ADR 0104 amendment of 2026-11-11: issuer: joins the pinned server fields"
---

Shipped in the PDF parser fork and defects run (docs/plans/2026-11-11 - 00 - pdf-parser-fork-and-defects-plan.md, items 11 to 13). It amends decisions 2, 9 and 11 for a new MCP server key, `issuer:`, which pins the OAuth authorization server a client secret may be sent to (ADR 0095 amendment `adr-0095-amend-1111`).

Decision 2 is unchanged in rule — the whole MCP server entry is pinned — and `issuer:` is one of its pinned fields (`internal/config/execpin.go`), so a changed issuer asks again. Decision 11's field list gains it: the pane shows `auth:`, `client-id:`, `client-secret-env:` and `issuer:`, in that order, after the headers, the issuer drawn as its URL. Decision 9 reads: an adopted repo entry may use `client-secret-env:` only beside an `issuer:`, and the pane names the endpoint, every variable it sends and the issuer the secret may go to; that is the gate.
