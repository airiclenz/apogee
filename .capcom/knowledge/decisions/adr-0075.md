---
title: The headless Event lines are a versioned Driver protocol over the engine's Event stream
---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

Headless Event lines are a versioned Driver protocol over the engine's Event stream: a separate
snake_case encoder carries every variant but the Inspector's, lossless, ordered and unscrubbed,
versioned `v:1` per line, additive within it, and pinned by goldens. Read `adr-0075-context`,
`adr-0075-decision`, `adr-0075-consequences`, then `adr-0075-rejected`.
