---
title: "ADR 0043 amendment of 2026-10-10: APOGEE_NO_UPDATE_CHECK is applied by hand"
---

One override does not go through its registry row: `APOGEE_NO_UPDATE_CHECK` (ADR 0097). Any non-empty value forces `update-check` off; the variable never spells the key's value. As the row's `EnvVar` it would be wrong — `applyEnv` hands the variable's text to the row's bool `Set`, so `1` would turn the check ON. The `update-check` row therefore names no variable; `applyNoUpdateCheck` (`internal/config/config.go`) applies it right after `applyEnv`, ranking above both config files with no flag to outrank it, and `overrideSources` records its /settings `env` marker from the same predicate, since its loop over the rows cannot see it. Rejected: a row `EnvVar` with an inverted `Set` (a second parser on one row). A future "force off" variable follows the same pattern and is named here. (2026-10-10, docs/plans/archived/2026-10-10 - 00 - windows-channels-and-update-notice-plan.md)
