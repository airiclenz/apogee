---
title: The engine runs workflows the model or a recipe asks for
---

Status: accepted
Amends: ADR 0039 (the 2026-09-20 fan-out ceiling now binds `sub_agent` alone), ADR 0022 D8 (a workflow item's conversation is kept in its workflow folder), ADR 0034 (`run: workflow:` arrives), ADR 0031 invariant 4 (the thin workflow layer is built)

The engine runs a Workflow, stages of items each done by a fresh child that hands back a checked
`finish` Receipt, only when asked: by the model's `fan_out` call, a new tool that ships off until
the bench switches it on, or by a Recipe in a Skill header. Workflows live under Scratch, their
children do not delegate, and the library is Driver-free. Read `adr-0087-context`,
`adr-0087-decision`, `adr-0087-rejected`, then `adr-0087-consequences`.
