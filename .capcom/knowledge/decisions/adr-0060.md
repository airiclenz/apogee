---
title: Effort is detected passively, dialected per server, and picked from the model's own levels
---

Status: accepted

Effort support is detected passively from `/props` and `/v1/models`, the wire dialect follows the
detection source (three are sighted) or a per-server `effort-dialect:` key, and `/effort` is a popup
offering the model's own reported levels, hidden where effort is unsupported. Read
`adr-0060-context`, `adr-0060-decision`, `adr-0060-rejected`, then `adr-0060-consequences`.
