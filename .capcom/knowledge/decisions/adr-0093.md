---
title: Usage is priced per call at the bound server
---

Status: accepted

Every provider call is priced when its usage is recorded, at the `price:` of the server bound to
that call, under one root `currency:` label in exact integer millionths; unpriced calls are counted,
never guessed, and a persisted amount keeps its label. The 2026-10-04 amendment keeps a resumed
session's own label. Read `adr-0093-context`, `adr-0093-decision`, `adr-0093-rejected`,
`adr-0093-consequences`, then `adr-0093-amend-1004`.
