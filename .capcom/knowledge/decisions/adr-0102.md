---
title: A plan owns an issue through the plan field, never a status
---

Status: accepted

When a plan under `docs/plans/` takes ownership of an issue, the issue records it in capcom's
plan field (`capcom update <id> --plan '<plan basename>'`), never as a status.
Read `adr-0102-context`, `adr-0102-decision`, `adr-0102-rejected`, then `adr-0102-consequences`.
