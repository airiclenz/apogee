---
title: The heartbeat observes the Upstream; rebind applies the change at the boundary
---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-02): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

The Heartbeat observes the Upstream on a fixed cadence and reports a Beat (reachability, the active
model, the context window and the `/v1/models` offering), and an idle-only rebind applies a changed
binding at the boundary, so cold start, a late seed and a mid-session switch are one asynchronous
path. A configured `context-window:` is a pin; a configured model is a hint. Read
`adr-0024-context`, `adr-0024-decision`, `adr-0024-rejected`, then `adr-0024-consequences`.
