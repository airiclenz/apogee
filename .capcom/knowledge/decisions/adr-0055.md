---
title: Daemon Schedules bind to named servers and never actuate a model load
---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-02): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

A daemon schedule binds to a `servers:` entry by name and inherits all it states; `run: model:` is a
per-request selection, refused on a launcher-fronted entry; the daemon never actuates the launcher,
and `schedules.yaml` never carries a raw endpoint or key. Read `adr-0055-context`,
`adr-0055-decision`, `adr-0055-rejected`, then `adr-0055-consequences`.
