> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

> **Superseded by ADR 0076 (2026-09-07; noted 2026-09-24).** Its decision 1 deletes the Mechanism registry
> this record designed; the text below is retained as the historical record.

A Mechanism was a self-contained module in a registry, ordered at each hook point by a deterministic
sort of its declared constraints with a stable tiebreak by ID, never a hardcoded pipeline. ADR 0076
deleted the registry, so nothing here binds the shipped engine; it stands as history. Read
`adr-0003-context`, `adr-0003-decision`, `adr-0003-consequences`, then `adr-0003-amend-0725`.
