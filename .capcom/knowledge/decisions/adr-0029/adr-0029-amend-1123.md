---
title: "ADR 0029 amendment of 2026-11-23: every Exchange opener waits out a refusing hold; the flush and the wake retry at its release"
---

Decision 5 says that while the actuation latch is held "sends ... are refused with a one-line note".
The code read "sends" as the human's sends only: a typed message, the held-queue `⏎`, `/continue`
and `/compact` were refused, while the two automatic openers were not. A completion flush (ADR 0025
D7) or a background-workflow wake (ADR 0089 D3) could open an Exchange into a server a launcher
verb was restarting — a `/stop-server` or `/model <profile>` queued mid-run and drained at the
completion, or the start-up restore, or a workflow ending during a load. Owner call, 2026-11-23:

- **"Sends" covers every opener.** No Exchange opens while a refusing hold stands. The refusing
  holds are named once (`holdsRefusingExchange` in `internal/tui/engineholds.go`): a session load,
  a `/bg` launch, a footer git action, and this latch.
- **The human openers refuse with the hold's note, as before.** The typed line stays in the box
  and `⏎` sends it once the hold has released.
- **The completion flush notes once and retries at the release.** A flush turned away by any
  refusing hold leaves the staged rows where they are under that hold's note, said once, and the
  Update tail sends them at the first fold that finds the hold released, the session idle with
  nothing queued ahead of them, and an upstream to send to (`flushAfterFold`). Rows held by a stop
  or a fault are not retried: they still wait for `⏎`.
- **The wake waits silently.** A pending wake fires at the first fold after the release that
  finds the engine free (`canOpenWake`), with no note.
- **After this latch, both wait for a beat.** A `/stop-server` or `/unload-model` releases the
  latch while the heartbeat, its failures ignored in the latch's shadow, still calls the server
  online. The flush retry and the wake therefore also wait until a beat has folded since the
  release (`actuation.beatOwed`), and the flush retry then asks `blockedUpstream` like a human
  opener.

This supersedes, for the retry, two statements of ADR 0025: decision 7's "a natural completion
auto-sends what is left" (`adr-0025-decision`), which now holds only when no refusing hold stands
at the completion, and `adr-0025-consequences`' "the auto-flush is a fourth opener and is
deliberately not gated" — the first try at the completion is still not gated on `blockedUpstream`,
for the reason that bullet gives, but the retry is. Recorded in
`docs/plans/2026-11-23 - 06 - architecture-review-cleanups-plan.md`, item 12.
