---
title: ADR 0102 context
---

Issues move through two kinds of work before they close. Design work writes a plan under
`docs/plans/` (numbered What/Tests/Acceptance/commit items), and implementation executes it.
The register needs to answer two questions: which issues are designed and ready to hand to
`/implement-plan`, and which still need design. It also needs to say which plan owns an issue.

The question came up when the register moved to capcom (2026-10-12). AGENTS.md carried the
ruling until this record took it over (2026-10-14).
