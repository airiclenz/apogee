A sub-agent is a nested `Agent` at the `sub_agent` recursion point, built by one orchestrator that
threads the parent's bounds, with isolated guard state. §5(b) gave way to ADR 0088, the depth bound
to `Config.Delegation.MaxDepth`. Read `adr-0013-context`, `adr-0013-decision`, `adr-0013-rejected`,
`adr-0013-consequences`, `adr-0013-post-v1`, `adr-0013-clarification`, `adr-0013-amend-0802`,
`adr-0013-amend-0807`, `adr-0013-amend-0918`, then `adr-0013-amend-1002`.
