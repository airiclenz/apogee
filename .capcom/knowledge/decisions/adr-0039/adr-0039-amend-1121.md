---
title: "ADR 0039 amendment of 2026-11-21: decision 4's recover boundary and ledger row are the child bracket's"
refs: [internal/agent/childrun.go]
---

**Why now.** Plan `2026-11-21 - 00 - delegation-one-value-plan`, items 7–12 (issue
`delegation-endings-hand-bracketed`). Each path a delegation could end on used to bracket itself by
hand, with its own recover frame, its own phase pair, its own ledger write and its own reading of the
ending, so the paths drifted apart. They now go through one bracket.

**What this restates.** Decision 4's "panic recovery at its own boundary", and the 2026-09-20
note's "`refusePastCeiling`'s own write" for the `refused` row of a call past the fan-out ceiling.
Each is to be read as this amendment restates it.

**(a) The recover boundary is the child bracket.** Every delegation is booked under one bracket,
`childBracket` (`internal/agent/childrun.go`), opened by the Agent that spawns the child: a
dispatched `sub_agent` call at any width (`runDelegation`), a `sub_agent` call run outside a
dispatch (`runSubAgent`, a bracket with no phases and no ledger), and a workflow item's child
(`workflowSpawner.Spawn`, from its run-id mint on, with no ledger). The bracket's `run` holds the
recover: a panic anywhere in the child's life stops there, is reported as an `ErrorEvent` naming
it, and becomes the error result that path answers a panicked delegation with — each path keeps
its own text. Its siblings and the parent Exchange survive it, as decision 4 promises. No caller
keeps a recover frame of its own, and the boundary is the same at every width.

**(b) The bracket books the one ledger row.** The bracket decides the delegation's ending once
(`settle`) and books its one delegate-ledger row from it. A delegation that ran, one refused inside
the run path before its child was built, and one settled before its child started — skipped for a
message sent now, refused past the fan-out ceiling, caught queued by a cancel, or stopped while
queued (`neverStarted`) — all book their rows there. The refused row of the 2026-09-20 note is the
bracket's write, opened by `refusePastCeiling`. A call the resolver refuses before dispatch (the
depth bound) books no row, as before, and a workflow item's bracket books none.

**(c) A child that never ran reads `refused`.** Undelivered mail held for a delegation whose child
never ran is reported with the reason `refused`, on the `sub_agent` and the workflow-spawn path
alike; on the workflow-spawn path it read `faulted` before (owner, 2026-11-21). The public wording
of `refused` is "the child refused it at the boundary, or never ran".

**What stands.** Decision 4's per-child contract, its independence of failures, and the cancel and
stop rules of the notes under it are unchanged. The ending is a return value of the frame that
opened the bracket, never a field on `Agent` (ADR 0083 part 3, noted 2026-11-21).
