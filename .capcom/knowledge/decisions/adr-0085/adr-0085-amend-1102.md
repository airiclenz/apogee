title: "ADR 0085 amendment of 2026-11-02: the fallback pools future Note is realised by ADR 0105"
---

Decided in the 2026-11-02 grill (docs/plans/2026-11-02 - 00 - midrun-model-fallback-plan.md, item 1). The fallback pools of `adr-0085-future` are realised by ADR 0105, and that Note now reads as the record of how the question was framed. ADR 0105 takes its terms as written. A `servers:` entry names `fallback:` entries. ADR 0082's spent re-stream budget is the trigger. Mid-Turn rehoming is defined explicitly rather than limiting the list to equivalent models (`adr-0028-amend-1102`). ADR 0047 is cited and does not conflict: the chain is across servers, not key sources. The engine stays wire-silent (ADR 0031): it is handed a resolver and the specs that resolver returns, never config.

Decision 1 stands. A fallover is not routing: it chooses nothing by request content, cost or load. It follows an owner-written list, only after the seat has failed. Decisions 4–6 also stand: a fallen-over seat's attempts are sampled as every attempt is, keyed by the fallback entry's name, so the stats show the outage and the move.
