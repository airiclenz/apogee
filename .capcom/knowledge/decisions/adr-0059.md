A Console is a persistent program the model drives across Turns, live host state that no snapshot or
resume inherits; `console_open` and `console_send` carry the Subprocess marker, `console_read` and
`console_close` sit on the read-only floor, and the family ships default-off, profile-enabled. Read
`adr-0059-context`, `adr-0059-decision`, `adr-0059-bounds`, `adr-0059-consequences`, then
`adr-0059-amend-1004` and `adr-0059-amend-1004b`.
