---
title: A silent stream is cut, and a transient fault is ridden out under a budget
superseded-by: [adr-0088]
---

Status: accepted; "a cancel retains nothing" and the rejected "retaining a cancelled child" superseded by ADR 0088 D2
Amends: ADR 0039 decision 4 (a faulted child's result carries a continue line and a draft note, and the child is retained); ADR 0013's 2026-09-18 amendment (retention is the capped OR faulted child's); ADR 0046 decision 4's 2026-09-19 note (the `restreamSpent` latch is a per-Turn budget)

An upstream silent past `stream-idle-timeout:` (default 10m) is cut as a transient fault; a Turn
re-streams a transient fault under a per-Turn budget with growing backoff, and a faulted delegate is
retained as a capped one is. All of it is floor, engine-side and Bypass-neutral. ADR 0088 D2
superseded "a cancel retains nothing"; the rest holds. Read `adr-0082-context`, `adr-0082-decision`,
`adr-0082-rejected`, then `adr-0082-consequences`.
