---
title: The local-platform north star binds every future layer to the embeddable engine
---

Status: accepted

The platform north star is engine-centric: the embeddable engine must stay sufficient for any
Driver, guarded by four door-keeping invariants (a wire-silent engine, a wait-tolerant Approver, no
first-party connectors, benchable all the way up). Its force is a tiebreaker: it obligates no
building, and a change that closes a door supersedes it. Read `adr-0031-context`,
`adr-0031-decision`, `adr-0031-rejected`, `adr-0031-consequences`, then `adr-0031-amend-0907`.
