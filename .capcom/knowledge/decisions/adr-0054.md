---
title: Options groups host capabilities into named interfaces, one per family
---

Status: accepted

A host capability that is a family of acts joins `Options` as one named interface, its contract
stated once on the interface; a nil host is the family's unwired degrade, a wired host that cannot
perform one act says so in that act's answer, and what is not a family stays a field. Read
`adr-0054-context`, `adr-0054-decision`, `adr-0054-consequences`, then `adr-0054-amend-0917`.
