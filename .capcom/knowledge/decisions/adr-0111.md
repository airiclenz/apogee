Status: accepted (grill 2026-11-08, after the verified peer comparison `docs/design/side-panel-findings.md`); amends ADR 0110 — supersedes its decisions 3 (the panel takes no keyboard focus) and 8 (the key binding waits), and designs the MCP section its decision 2 named; amended by `adr-0111-amend-1109` (2026-11-09: an MCP server that dies mid-session is noticed and reads `disconnected (lost)`, answering the consequence that a mid-session death is not detected), by `adr-0111-amend-1109b` (2026-11-09: a side-panel header is two stops — its pin cell's `⏎` toggles the pin, its title's folds the section — superseding Decision 4's header-toggles-pin sentence) and by `adr-0111-amend-1109c` (2026-11-09: the `mcp` section's rows right-align `state · N tools`, superseding Decision 10's `name · state · N tools`, and the panel and `/mcp` paint `+N −M`, `done` and every failure in the transcript's outcome tones)

The block cursor grows into one **navigation mode** over two regions, the transcript and the side
panel: `alt+↑`/`alt+↓` enter it, `→` and `←` move between the regions, and `↑`/`↓`, `⏎`, `esc`
and `^x` mean the same in both. The panel's stops are its click targets, and `⏎` acts through the
click's own path, so key and pointer cannot drift. On a window too narrow for the docked panel,
`→` (or bare `/panel`) opens it as an overlay. Both cursors gain a mark that is not colour. MCP
server lifecycle gets a journaled engine Event, which feeds a read-only `/mcp` report pane and a
fifth, `auto` panel section. A sub-agent row reads `waiting` while its run has an approval
outstanding. With this, the IDEAS item "complete key-based navigation — including side-ways arrow
keys" is designed. Read `adr-0111-context`, `adr-0111-decision`, `adr-0111-rejected`, `adr-0111-consequences`,
then `adr-0111-amend-1109`, `adr-0111-amend-1109b` and `adr-0111-amend-1109c`.
