---
title: Live sessions are watchable through a Driver-written event journal
---

Status: accepted; decision 4 qualified on Windows (the release may leave the journal for the startup sweep) by `adr-0103-amend-1017`; decision 3 qualified (the journal's `run_started` also names the run's `model` and `context_window`) by `adr-0103-amend-1102`

While a session runs, the Driver running it writes a per-session Event journal beside its record:
Event lines in the `v:2` envelope with token and reasoning deltas coalesced, removed when the
record is released. The TUI's Watch mode paints the record's committed entries and then follows
that journal, read-only. It drives no loop, never holds, saves or writes, and opens from
`/sessions` or `--resume <id>`. Once configured, the homes of other users are watchable the same
way, as read-only sources. Read `adr-0103-context`, `adr-0103-decision`, `adr-0103-rejected`, then
`adr-0103-consequences`, `adr-0103-amend-1017` and `adr-0103-amend-1102`.
