---
title: Thinking effort is a profile axis with one canonical wire mapping
---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

Thinking effort is the emit-side leaf of the Model profile's thinking axis, `off` to `high`, mapped
to the wire once by the provider Client, with `/effort` as a session override above the profile; it
is configuration that holds under Bypass, and `DisableThinking` is deleted. ADR 0060 made the
mapping one of three dialects. Read `adr-0050-context`, `adr-0050-decision`, `adr-0050-rejected`,
then `adr-0050-deferred`.
