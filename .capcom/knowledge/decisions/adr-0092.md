---
title: Replayed signed thinking is guarded by a prefix digest
---

Status: accepted
Amends: ADR 0078 (Amendment 2026-10-02: on the preserved-thinking models a reply's signed thinking blocks go back only over the prefix they were produced on)

A reply's signed thinking blocks are replayed upstream only when the request prefix before that
reply digests identically to the one it was produced on; otherwise the codec drops them client-side
and never fails the request. The digest is computed per request at encode time, an entry without one
is dropped, and the drop cascades. Read `adr-0092-context`, `adr-0092-decision`,
`adr-0092-rejected`, then `adr-0092-consequences`.
