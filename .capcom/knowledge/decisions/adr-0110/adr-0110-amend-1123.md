---
title: "ADR 0110 amendment of 2026-11-23: a separator drag re-wraps once per settled burst of motions"
refs: [docs/layout/side-panel-layout.md, internal/tui/sidepanel.go, internal/tui/model.go]
---

**Why now.** A separator drag relayouted the frame on every mouse motion that moved the width
(`adr-0110-amend-1108` (b), (e)), and each relayout is a cold repaint of the whole scrollback at a
new width. A terminal reports motion faster than a long transcript repaints, so the motions queue and
each one is painted in turn, long after the pointer has moved on (issue `panel-drag-cold-repaints`,
the TUI performance audit of 2026-11-23). The owner ratified coalescing the queued motions to one
repaint at the latest width (plan `2026-11-23 - 08 - tui-perf-repaint-plan.md`, item 8).

**What this supersedes.** `adr-0110-amend-1108` (b)'s "the panel and the transcript re-wrap live on
every column it moves" and (e)'s exception for "a held-button mouse motion while a separator drag
is active". Each is to be read as this amendment restates it.

**(a) The drag re-wraps once per settled burst.** The panel and the transcript re-wrap live while the
separator is dragged, once for every burst of motions the terminal has queued, at the latest
motion's width. A motion records the width it lands as the drag's target; the first that moves it
off the width standing schedules one settle Msg, and every motion folded before that Msg lands only
moves the target. The settle applies the latest target, and the frame is laid out there. The release
applies any target still pending before it writes `ui.side-panel.width`, so the drag's final width
is the one persisted, as (c) of `adr-0110-amend-1108` states. Widths the queued motions passed
through are never drawn; each frame that is drawn is the one the old rule drew at that width.

**(b) No exception to "mouse motion never repaints".** Every mouse motion, a separator drag's
included, now never repaints: the drag repaints on its own settle Msg, which is not motion. The
settle is inert once the drag has ended (the release applied its target) or a resize that hid the
column abandoned it. The rule lives in `internal/tui/model.go` (`settleFrame`),
`internal/tui/sidepanel.go` (`dragPanelSeparator`, `panelDragSettleMsg`), `internal/tui/doc.go`,
`TestMouseMotionNeverRepaints`, `TestMouseMotionRepaintsADraggedSeparator` and
`TestDragBurstCostsOneColdRepaint`.
