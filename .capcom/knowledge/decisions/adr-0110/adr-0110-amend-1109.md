title: "ADR 0110 amendment of 2026-11-09: an overflow-squeezed section wears ▶ and opens into a session-only tier"
refs: [docs/layout/side-panel-layout.md, internal/tui/sidepanel.go]
---

**Why now.** Decision 11 folds a squeezed section to its header and stops there: the header gave
no sign that it hid rows, and a squeezed section could not be opened at all, since a click on its
header only wrote `ui.side-panel.folded` and the overflow squeezed it again on the next frame. The
side-panel visual alignment (plan
[`2026-11-09 - 04 - side-panel-visual-alignment-plan.md`](../../../../docs/plans/2026-11-09%20-%2004%20-%20side-panel-visual-alignment-plan.md),
issue `panel-overflow-fold-marks`) gave every header the transcript's fold indicator, and the
owner ratified on 2026-11-09 ("Overflow" in the plan's ratified design calls) that a squeezed
section wears it and opens into a session-only tier.

**What this supersedes.** Decision 11's "pinned sections get rows first, then auto sections in list
order" gains a middle tier, and its "folds to a one-line summary" now carries the fold mark. "Overflow
folds, never scrolls" and "the panel does not scroll" hold.

**(a) The fold mark.** A header's fold indicator reads `▶` exactly when the section has body rows
the column is not showing — the human folded it (`ui.side-panel.folded`) or the overflow squeezed
it — and `▼` otherwise. A section with no body rows (a pinned changed-files section before any
write) never reads `▶`, folded or not. A folded header carries no hidden-row count. The
`+N more` row paints in the transcript's `tool-marker` role, the role of its `+N more lines`
remainder.

**(b) The opened tier.** A click on a squeezed section's header (or `⏎` on its title stop) opens it
into the session's **opened tier**, which takes rows after the pinned sections and before the auto
ones, each in list order: the auto sections then share what is left, so the rows it takes come out
of the auto sections after it — the last auto section in list order is squeezed first. A click on
an opened section folds it, writes the fold to `ui.side-panel.folded` and drops it from the tier.
Unfolding a section the human folded also opens it into the tier, so the overflow cannot squeeze it
straight back and one click always shows a change. The tier is never written to config: it lasts
until apogee exits, and a restarted session starts without it.
