---
title: Windows confinement is a restricted low-integrity token, and the box is a label on the disk
---

Status: accepted

On Windows a confined child runs under a restricted, Low-integrity token: the integrity check fences
writes, the box is a Low label on the workspace and writable paths on disk, and no helper process
exists. Network egress is always reported unconfined. Read `adr-0020-context`, `adr-0020-decision`,
`adr-0020-rejected`, `adr-0020-consequences`, then `adr-0020-amend-0829`.
