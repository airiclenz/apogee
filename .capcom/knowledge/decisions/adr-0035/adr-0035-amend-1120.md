**Why now.** Under `ui.tools-auto-collapse` a settled, untouched `✦ Tools` umbrella folded to its
one Group summary line, but three other tool surfaces stayed open once their work was over: a lone
tool call (`✦ Read` over its `┕ README.md … 249 lines` row), the `✦ Sub-agents (N)` group and the
`✦ Skill (N)` group. A transcript of finished work read half folded, and a folded umbrella's
uncoloured `✦` was hard to tell from narration (issue `lone-tools-never-fold`, owner report and
decisions 2026-11-20, settling the follow-up `settled-group-collapse-scope`).

**What this supersedes.** The **Scope** paragraph of `adr-0035-amend-1118` ("`✦ Tools` umbrellas
only. Sub-agent groups, Skill groups and lone tool blocks keep their headers and folds"). Its (a)
to (d) stand as written for umbrellas and are to be read as extended below; the settings key, its
default, its live apply, decision 2's fence (auto-collapse writes nothing) and the session record
are unchanged.

**(a) Scope: every settled tool block that groups or could.** With `ui.tools-auto-collapse` on, a
**settled** block the reader has not **touched** folds to one line, in four shapes: a `✦ Tools`
umbrella (as shipped), a **lone groupable tool call** (a call that could be an umbrella's member
yet heads none and joins none), a `✦ Sub-agents (N)` group and a `✦ Skill (N)` group. *Settled*
and *touched* keep `adr-0035-amend-1118`'s meaning, read per block: every member of it has
finished (a call returned, a delegation's child reported) and a later entry of its own run follows
it or its run is over; touched is in-session view state, never serialized, so a resumed session
shows every such block folded. Off, nothing changes from the rule before this amendment. Not in
scope, and never auto-collapsed: a solo call that is not groupable (a lone skill fetch, a lone
delegation run block, an answered question), a task-list card (its own `ui.task-list-open` fold),
a workflow block.

**(b) A lone call folds to the Group summary of one (owner).** Its folded line is exactly the
header an umbrella of that one call paints folded: `✦ Read 1 file ⋯ ▶`, `✦ Ran 1 command ⋯ ▶`.
A click or `enter` there opens it to the block it painted before, exactly; it is then touched and
stays open. A lone call has no fold but the settled one: it grows no header toggle, and every
click on its open block keeps the meaning it had. Its touch is its own mark, apart from an
umbrella's, so a touch never carries between the two shapes one head can take.

**(c) A group header is always a fold (owner).** A Sub-agent or Skill group's header is a leader
row in the umbrella header's shape, its dots running to a `▼` (open) or `▶` (folded) at the
block's edge, and a click or `enter` on it folds the group to that header line alone and back, on
a session-only flag on its head, live or settled, auto-collapse on or off — a small umbrella's
model. Settled and untouched under auto-collapse, the group is folded to that line; a click opens
it alone on a fold of its own, touched, and writes nothing. Opening a member — a delegation's run
view included — or jumping to one from the side panel touches the group.

**(d) Every tool block's star is the tools header colour (owner).** The leading `✦` of every tool
block's header — umbrellas, lone calls (task-list cards and lone Skill and delegation blocks
included), Sub-agent and Skill groups — paints in the scheme's `tool-header` role, not bold, so a
folded line reads as a tool block; the live blink and a Firing's `⟳` are unchanged.

All of it is TUI view state and paint: no engine, `domain.ToolSummary`, config-key or
session-record change, so the engine stays Driver-neutral (ADR 0031) and nothing reaches the
model. Spec: `docs/layout/tool-layout.md`, "Fold states and interaction" and the folded sketches;
plan `docs/plans/2026-11-20 - 00 - settled-tool-blocks-fold-plan.md` carries the ratified calls.
