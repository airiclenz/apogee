title: "ADR 0035 amendment of 2026-11-18: settled Tools umbrellas auto-collapse above the size rule"
refs: [docs/layout/tool-layout.md, internal/tui/transcript.go, internal/tui/groupsummary.go]
---

**Why now.** A finished `✦ Tools` umbrella stayed open in the transcript long after the
conversation had moved past it: a small one starts every session open and stays open until the
reader folds it by hand, so a long session fills with groups nobody is reading. The owner asked
for a group that is no longer active to collapse to one row that says in plain words what it did
(issue `settled-tool-group-clutter`, owner decisions 2026-11-18).

**What this supersedes.** `adr-0035-addendum-0918`'s "size alone says whose state the fold is",
for SETTLED umbrellas only: size still decides a live umbrella and a touched one, exactly as that
addendum states it. `adr-0035-addendum-0917`'s click — folding or opening every large umbrella and
writing `ui.tools-open` — for an umbrella auto-collapse is holding: that click opens the one
umbrella alone and writes nothing. Both addenda stand otherwise, and are to be read as this
amendment restates them.

**(a) Auto-collapse sits above the size rule.** A new registry-declared, editable bool,
`ui.tools-auto-collapse` (default `true`), shown in `/settings` and applied live from a hand-edit.
While it is on, a **settled** umbrella the reader has not **touched** is folded to its header line
whatever its size and whatever `ui.tools-open` says. *Settled* is derived at paint and stored
nowhere: every call in the umbrella has returned, AND either a later entry of its own run follows
it (a narration or reply, a note, a user message, a further block) or nothing follows and its run
is no longer running: the human's Turn for the conversation, the child for a sub-agent's run view
(owner: the same rule applies inside a run view). A replayed umbrella is therefore settled. Off,
every umbrella folds by its size as the 2026-09-18 addendum says, and nothing else changes.

**(b) "Touched" is view state.** The reader touches an umbrella by moving its header's fold (a
click, or `enter` at the block cursor), by opening a type row, a member or a member's body in it,
or by jumping to one of its members from the side panel. A touched umbrella is exempt from
auto-collapse for the rest of the session and folds by its size again. The mark is in-session
only and never serialized, like a small umbrella's fold, so a resumed session shows every settled
umbrella collapsed.

**(c) An auto-collapsed umbrella opens alone (owner).** A click on an umbrella auto-collapse is
holding opens that one umbrella on a fold of its own head flag: touched, kept at any size, and
flipped by every later click. It writes nothing, not `ui.tools-open`, so the set of
program-written keys does not grow and decision 2's fence is untouched: auto-collapse records no
gesture. A click on a live or touched large umbrella keeps the 2026-09-17 addendum's global
toggle and silent write.

**(d) Every umbrella header is its Group summary (owner).** The header is no longer the
tool-type breakdown `Tools (3 Read, 4 Terminal)` but the umbrella's **Group summary**: verb
phrases built from each call's view and Tool summary, in the order each kind first ran
(`Read 3 files, ran 4 commands, edited 2 files +18 −5 · 1 failed`), with the edits' summed
diffstat, the failed and denied counts, a live verb while a call of a kind is in flight, and a
width fallback that ends at `Tools (N calls)`. The four writing tools all read "edited N files"
(distinct paths); there is no "created" (owner). A tool with no phrase reads `<Label> ×N`. The
summary is built in the TUI; no engine, `domain.ToolSummary` or session-record change, so the
engine stays Driver-neutral (ADR 0031). Nothing reaches the model, so the Floor and Bypass are
not involved. The full spec is `docs/layout/tool-layout.md`, Vocabulary and "Fold states and
interaction".

**Scope.** `✦ Tools` umbrellas only. Sub-agent groups, Skill groups and lone tool blocks keep
their headers and folds (follow-up issue `settled-group-collapse-scope`). Plan
`docs/plans/2026-11-18 - 00 - settled-tools-and-watch-panel-width-plan.md` carries the ratified
calls.
