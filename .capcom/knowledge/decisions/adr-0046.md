---
title: The engine bounds every reply with an output-token cap
---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

Every request carries a `MaxTokens` cap derived from the Budget's `ResponseReserve`, clamped to
[4096, 32768] and pinnable per server with `max-output-tokens:`; the cap is engine behaviour that
holds in Bypass, and a reply cut off at it fails with a message that names it. Read
`adr-0046-context`, `adr-0046-decision`, `adr-0046-rejected`, `adr-0046-consequences`, then
`adr-0046-amend-0813` and `adr-0046-addendum-0826`.
