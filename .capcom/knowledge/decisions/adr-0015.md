> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

> **Superseded by ADR 0076 (2026-09-07; noted 2026-09-24).** Its decision 1 deletes the catalogue and
> `Config.EnableMechanisms`; the text below is retained as the historical record.

`Config.EnableMechanisms` armed catalogued Mechanisms by ID for the CLI, the bench and embedders
alike, with public descriptors the bench planned its arms from. ADR 0076 deleted the catalogue and
the field, so nothing here binds shipped code; it stands as history. Read `adr-0015-context`,
`adr-0015-decision`, `adr-0015-rejected`, `adr-0015-consequences`, then `adr-0015-realisation`.
