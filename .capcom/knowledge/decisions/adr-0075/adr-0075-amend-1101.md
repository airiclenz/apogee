title: "ADR 0075 amendment of 2026-11-01: a context_estimate kind and a per-result token estimate"
---

Two additions, both within `v:2` under decision 10 — a kind or member a reader does not know is one it ignores, so neither bumps the version.

**A twenty-fifth kind, `context_estimate`.** Decision 4's list gains `context_estimate`, written for `domain.ContextEstimateEvent`: the engine's estimate of the emitting agent's context fill after a tool result joins its conversation, at that agent's depth. `data.tokens` is the last reading at that depth (prompt plus completion of the last non-maintenance `usage`) plus the estimated tokens of every message appended since — before any reading, the agent's own context estimate — and `data.calibrated` says whether the estimator had been calibrated against a real reading. It is an estimate, never a reading: the next `usage` line at the same depth supersedes it, and it adds nothing to the cumulative totals. The vocabulary is twenty-five kinds — twenty-three Event variants plus the two frames — with the new kind placed before the frames in `Kinds()`.

**`tool_result` gains `data.estimated_tokens`.** The engine's estimate of the tokens that result's content adds to the emitting agent's context (`domain.ToolResultEvent.EstimatedTokens`). Per decision 3 it is always present — `0` where no estimate was made — never omitted. It joins after `spawn_run_id`, so the decision 8 amendment of 2026-09-24 that calls `data.spawn_run_id` "always present and last" on `tool_call` and `tool_result` now holds "last" for `tool_call` only; on `tool_result`, `spawn_run_id` is followed by `estimated_tokens`.
