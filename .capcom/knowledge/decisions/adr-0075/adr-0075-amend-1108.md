It follows that call's `context_estimate` line at the same depth. The `/clear` boundary and a resume write none: a Driver resets its fold at `/clear` and rebuilds it on resume from the stored entries. The vocabulary is twenty-seven kinds, twenty-five Event variants plus the two frames, with the new kind placed before the frames in `Kinds()`.
