Two additions within `v:2` under decision 10. A kind or a member a reader does not know is one it ignores, so neither bumps the version.

**A twenty-seventh kind, `task_list`.** Decision 4's list gains `task_list`, written for `domain.TaskListEvent`: the emitting agent's task list as a successful `task_list` tool call left it (ADR 0072). `data.items` is the whole list in the model's order, each row a `text` and a `done`, and `[]` for a list the call cleared, never `null`. Each call replaces the whole list, so the line carries the list, never a delta. A delegation keeps a list of its own, so the line is written at every depth with the emitting agent's identity. It follows that call's `context_estimate` line at the same depth. The `/clear` boundary and a resume write none: a Driver resets its fold at `/clear` and rebuilds it on resume from the stored entries. The vocabulary is twenty-seven kinds, twenty-five Event variants plus the two frames, with the new kind placed before the frames in `Kinds()`. This supersedes the "twenty-six" of `adr-0075-amend-1102` by amendment, not by rewrite.

(2026-11-08, docs/plans/2026-11-08 - 00 - tui-side-panel-plan.md, item 1)

**`sub_agent_phase` gains `data.at`.** The moment the delegation crossed the phase (`domain.SubAgentPhaseEvent.At`), as an RFC3339Nano string, on both `started` and `finished`, so any Driver can compute a run's elapsed time without a clock of its own. The engine stamps it once, where every phase is emitted, from the emitting agent's clock. Per decision 3 it is always present, never omitted: `null` where the event carries no time (the `run_finished` frame's `error` precedent). It joins last, after `cap_requested`. It is the engine's moment of the phase and can differ from the envelope's `time`, which is the moment the Driver wrote the line.
