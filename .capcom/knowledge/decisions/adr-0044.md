---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-02): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

The Model profile resolves per model through the user's `model-profiles:` map, then a shipped shape
table matched by case-insensitive substring and grown per sighting, then the zero profile, and rides
every switch through Rebind; the global `model-profile:` block is retired. ADR 0057 made decision
4's whole-profile replace axis-wise. Read `adr-0044-context`, `adr-0044-decision`,
`adr-0044-rejected`, `adr-0044-deferred`, then `adr-0044-consequences`.
