title: The launcher actuates local servers, and the beat completes every move
---

Status: accepted; decision 3's verbs and the top-level `llama-launcher:` key superseded by its own 2026-07-29 and 2026-08-07 amendments

The llama-launcher actuates local servers while the heartbeat observes them, and the beat completes
every move: the session follows the loaded profile, the launcher's config is the single profile
store, and one latch ignores beats in an actuation's shadow. Its own amendments fold the verbs into
`/model`, `/unload-model` and `/stop-server`, and move the launcher key onto the `servers:` entry.
Read `adr-0029-context`, `adr-0029-decision`, then `adr-0029-consequences`.
