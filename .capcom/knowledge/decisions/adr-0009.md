Status: accepted; superseded for structural behaviour (Floor guards) by ADR 0071

> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

Bench validation runs two tests: a mandatory one-sided non-inferiority gate against Bypass down to a
measured noise floor, and a separate superiority test that decides default-on, with the distinct
task as the paired unit. It still governs model-shaping behaviour; ADR 0071 took the structural
Floor guards out of its reach. Read `adr-0009-context`, `adr-0009-decision`,
`adr-0009-consequences`, then `adr-0009-refinements`.
