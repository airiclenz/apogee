---
title: Sub-agent privileges are bounded by the parent
superseded-by: [adr-0012, adr-0013]
---

Status: accepted; the "Auto sub-agent still requires Confinement" bullet superseded by ADR 0012, the audit guardrail by ADR 0013 (2026-10-02 amendment)

A sub-agent's privileges never exceed its parent's: it inherits the parent's Agent mode or a
stricter one, passes the same guardrails, and gets a subset of the parent's tools. The
Auto-needs-Confinement bullet gave way to ADR 0012 and the audit guardrail to ADR 0013. Read
`adr-0005-context`, `adr-0005-decision`, `adr-0005-consequences`, then `adr-0005-amend-1003`, where
an Auto sub-agent inherits the parent's blast radius.
