title: Shipped skills and the load_skill door
---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-02): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

Four skills ship embedded as the lowest-priority source, never installed, their files served through
a virtual read mount and switched by `use-shipped-skills`. `load_skill` is a default-on tool with an
adaptive single-call shape, the catalog stays out of the standing prompt, and B1 stays deferred.
Read `adr-0065-context`, `adr-0065-decision`, `adr-0065-rejected`, then `adr-0065-consequences`.
