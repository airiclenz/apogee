---
title: v1.0.0 is cut with a self-retracting placeholder above the burned v1.x numbers
---

Status: accepted

The v1.x numbers the Go module proxy and checksum database already hold — v1.1.0, v1.7.0, v1.8.0 —
are burned, and so is v1.8.1, the placeholder. go.mod retracts exactly those four by name, and
`internal/update` owns the list. Every other v1.x number, v1.0.0 included, is releasable. v1.0.0
is cut with the placeholder v1.8.1 tagged on its own commit: the placeholder outranks v1.8.0,
carries the list and retracts itself, so `go install …@latest` resolves to v1.0.0. A 0.x bridge
release ships the list-based update check first, and a bad placeholder is recovered by appending
the next unused v1.8.x. Read `adr-0107-context`, `adr-0107-decision`, `adr-0107-rejected`, then
`adr-0107-consequences`.
