---
title: "The context-fill notice is the first engine advise Reaction: a top-level boolean, scaled to the compaction line"
---

Status: accepted

`context-fill-notice` is an engine advise Reaction, off by default, firing once per climb at 50, 75
and 90 percent of the compaction line; the step- and token-budget notices became structural. Read
`adr-0077-context`, `adr-0077-decision`, `adr-0077-rejected`, `adr-0077-consequences`, then
`adr-0077-addendum-0915`, `adr-0077-addendum-0919`, `adr-0077-addendum-0919b` and
`adr-0077-addendum-0922`.
