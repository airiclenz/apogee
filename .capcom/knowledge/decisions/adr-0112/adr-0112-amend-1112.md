---
title: "ADR 0112 amendment of 2026-11-12: a residual inside the collapsed Session log is disclosed"
---

**Why now.** Plan `docs/plans/2026-11-12 - 00 - go127-gate-and-session-log-plan.md` gathers the
TUI's routine start-up notes into one **Session log** block, collapsed by default (issue
`startup-session-log`; the spec is `layout.md` §"The start-up box"). The Auto residual notice
(`probe.ResidualNotice`) is one of those notes. Decision 1 says "the operator is told what the fence
does not stop"; this amendment settles that a residual folded into the log still meets it.

**1. In the TUI, the residual notice is a routine Session log note.** It is present in the
transcript at every start that words it, counted in the log's `✦ Session log (N notes)` header and
one keypress (or one click) from view, verbatim. That satisfies decision 1's "the operator is told".
The residual is a known, standing fact of the backend, not an event that needs the human now, so it
is routine rather than loud. The notices that do need the human stay loud and keep their own line
outside the log: Auto running unconfined (`unconfinedAutoWarning`) and degraded Auto
(`probe.DegradedNotice`).

**2. stderr, headless and daemon are unchanged.** The residual notice still prints on stderr at
launch, and `apogee headless` and `apogee daemon` word it exactly as before, byte for byte. Only the
interactive TUI's transcript copy moves into the log.

**3. The transcript copy is not withdrawn.** This yields to closed issue `startup-notices-stderr`
(closed 2026-09-16, reason "startup confinement notices repeat in the transcript as ephemeral notes
(6d160f1e)"): the confinement notices moved into the transcript because the stderr line alone was
easy to miss. The log keeps them in the transcript — collapsed, never dropped — so that reason
still holds.

**Pinned by** the `TestSessionLog…` tests items 5 and 6 of that plan add (among them: an anomaly
stays its own note, and the log is never encoded).
