---
title: Double-press gestures disarm only on their reset tick
---

Status: accepted

The esc×2 stop and ctrl+c×2 quit gestures disarm only on their own reset tick (or, for esc, the
worker unwinding). The second press is never measured against a wall-clock window at the moment it
is folded, so a second press typed in time always acts, however late a loaded program folds it.
Read `adr-0099-context`, `adr-0099-decision`, `adr-0099-rejected`, then `adr-0099-consequences`.
