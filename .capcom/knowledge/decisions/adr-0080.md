---
title: git_commit forces a look at staged secret material
---

Status: accepted; decision 6's per-run 2 s timeout and silent skip on a mid-scan failure superseded by `adr-0080-amend-0923` (one 30 s budget; an incomplete scan forces the look)
Amends: ADR 0012 (the dangerous-action guard gains its first content-derived Tier-2 member, `commit-secrets`)

`git_commit` scans a shadow index of the call's files for secret material before it runs; a finding
forces a Tier-2 approval look under `commit-secrets` in every mode, named in the Hint. The amendment
gives one 30 s budget and forces the look on an incomplete scan. Read `adr-0080-context`,
`adr-0080-decision`, `adr-0080-consequences`, `adr-0080-rejected`, then `adr-0080-amend-0923`.
