---
title: "ADR 0018 amendment of 2026-11-02: a fresh tool result is bounded by the room left, and what lines cannot bound is byte-elided"
---

Decided by the owner on 2026-11-02, from a Seqcom run (20261102T1700Z) in which one minified
`web_fetch` body larger than the window overflowed the next request and then the emergency fold
meant to rescue it (issue `fresh-result-overflow`; plan
`docs/plans/2026-11-02 - 03 - fresh-result-overflow-plan.md`). Decisions 5 and 7 stand: one fold
per Turn is still the only retry, and the predictive guard still measures the advertised room.

**1. A fresh tool result is bounded by the room left in the request.** Decision 8's clamp at
`appendToolResult` no longer bounds by the whole History allocation alone. With a known window a
result is committed at no more than its **share of the room left**: the advertised window less the
reply reserve — the predictive guard's own room (decision 7) — less the estimate of the next request
without the result (the standing system content, the conversation, the tool menu), divided across the
Turn's results still to append. The share is recomputed at each append, so the room a small result
leaves unused rolls forward to the next. It is clamped to **[1/8 of the History allocation, the
History allocation]**: never above the ceiling decision 8's survivability ordering rests on, and
never below the floor, where the predictive guard folds the conversation instead of starving the
result. The estimate is decision 7's own measure with no comfort margin and no second estimator.
With no window known the bound is the History allocation measured against the fold's unknown-window
budget, exactly as before. The clamp stays in the loop, with no config key and on under Bypass
(decision 8, ADR 0006); the `tool-result-cap` Floor guard and its protected span are unchanged.

**2. What line elision cannot bound is byte-elided.** The shared rendering still elides head and tail
lines first (`context.TruncateToolResult`), but a body that comes out of it still over its bound — a
few very long lines, a minified JSON page — is cut to the bound in characters instead
(`context.ElideToBytes`, the same marker). Every structural clamp shares that rendering, so an
`@file` block and an attached skill body get the same fallback. This supersedes the
`adr-0018-consequences` bullet that the floor's rendering is **line-based** and bounds lines, not
characters, and that a body the head/tail form cannot shrink is passed through whole: the clamp now
holds for any input shape.

**3. The fold's transcript elides an oversized newest message.** Decision 8's premise that the
fold's summary call "keeps the most recent message unconditionally" no longer holds. The budgeted
transcript still keeps the most recent message, but one that alone exceeds the room left after the
protected prefix and the elision notice is kept head-and-tail with the elision marker, cut to that
room and never below a small floor (`newestMessageFloor`) so the summarizer always sees how it
starts. The fold no longer depends on the clamp to stay survivable; the clamp is still the intended
shape and the fold's elision the last-resort rescue.

**4. The raw-text price now covers ordinary results.** The `adr-0018-consequences` bullet that "the
raw text of a pathological result never reaches history, a snapshot, or the rendered transcript —
the accepted price" is superseded in scope: any result over its share of the room left — an
ordinary large read on a crowded request, not only one bigger than all of History — is committed
elided, and its raw text likewise never reaches history, a snapshot or the transcript. The price is
accepted on the same ground: the marker tells the model the middle was dropped, and the call can be
re-run for the part it needs.

**5. The provider classifies the OpenAI-shaped overflow.** The consequences' "`internal/provider`
unmodified (its classification was already correct)" no longer holds for one wire form: an HTTP 400
carrying `context_length_exceeded` or "exceeds the context window" is now a context overflow, so it
reaches this record's one emergency fold instead of abandoning the Turn as a plain fault.
