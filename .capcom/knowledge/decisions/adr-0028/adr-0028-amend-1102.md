---
title: "ADR 0028 amendment of 2026-11-02: a fallback rehome is the one mid-Turn switch"
---

Decided in the 2026-11-02 grill that produced ADR 0105 (docs/plans/2026-11-02 - 00 - midrun-model-fallback-plan.md, item 1). It amends decision 3's idle-only rule in one place. A **Server fallback** rehome (ADR 0105) is allowed mid-Turn. It is the only switch that is.

Decision 3 keeps `SwitchUpstream` idle-only "so no request is ever re-pointed at a different server underneath itself". The fallback rehome keeps that promise. The request it moves has already failed, and its partial response is discarded (a `stream_reset` precedes the resend). The failed request is then resent whole on the fallback. Nothing in flight is re-pointed. The rehome is applied by the agent's own loop at the request boundary, so the loop's un-mutexed `cfg` reads are synchronized by the same boundary decision 3 relies on. It takes decision 3's shape exactly: a fresh client from an `UpstreamSpec` carrying the fallback entry's endpoint, key and the two token bounds of `adr-0028-amend-0813`, the model unbound, and one beat on the fallback binding it through a `RebindSpec` before the resend.

What stands: `/server` and `/model` stay idle-only (decision 7, as amended), and a human switch is still the only one that records `server:` (decision 5 as amended by ADR 0036). A fallover writes nothing to `config.yaml`, so a resumed session starts on the configured primary. `/server` is also the door back from a fallen-over session seat.
