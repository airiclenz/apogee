`apogee daemon` is an in-repo subcommand, a separate runtime with no IPC, driven by a live-reloaded
declarative trigger-action file; Firings follow ADR 0033's posture, records go to the shared
sessions store, and v1 triggers are cycle only. ADR 0076 removed decision 8's catalogued-Mechanism
route and decision 10's resolver is still unbuilt. Read `adr-0034-context`, `adr-0034-decision`,
`adr-0034-rejected`, then `adr-0034-consequences`.
