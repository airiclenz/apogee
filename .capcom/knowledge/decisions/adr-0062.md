Test drivers are Drivers: they enter through the composition seam with no test-only engine hook,
`internal/stubllm` is the one scripted upstream, `charmbracelet/x/vt` is the frame authority
asserted on cells, and a gated LLM judge gives binding verdicts. Named proxies are the automated
definition of the irreducible checklist halves. Read `adr-0062-context`, `adr-0062-decision`, then
`adr-0062-consequences`.
