---
title: "The thinking axis resolves as two sub-axes: channel style and effort"
---

Status: accepted

> Note (2026-10-07): decision 4's "any of the four words" is ADR 0050's `off | low | medium | high`.
> ADR 0060 decision 4 widened the
> vocabulary to seven (`off | low | medium | high | minimal | xhigh | max`, plus `none` on the
> `reasoning` dialect); the reasoning here holds for every one of them.

The thinking axis resolves as two sub-axes, each through user, shipped, then zero: the channel style
`{Style, Start, End}`, whose tokens travel with the style, and `Effort` alone, so an effort-only
user entry keeps the shipped channel style. Read `adr-0058-context`, `adr-0058-decision`,
`adr-0058-bounds`, `adr-0058-rejected`, then `adr-0058-consequences`.
