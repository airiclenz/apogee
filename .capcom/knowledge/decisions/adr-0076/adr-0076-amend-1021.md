---
title: "ADR 0076 amendment of 2026-10-21: an admission arm gates what apogee ships on, not a user's own cell"
---

Decided by the owner on 2026-10-21 (issue `session-start-advise`). Decisions 2 and 6 stand as
first written; on the admission of a user cell this amendment supersedes decision 5's arm as a
gate on the user row, and the 2026-10-19 amendment's "has its own admission arm" bullet:

- **An admission arm gates what apogee ships on.** An engine-origin or bench-armed Reaction, and
  any slot apogee itself fills by default, passes a decision-5-style arm against Bypass (ADR 0009)
  before it ships on. That is the invariant's own scope: nothing *apogee* puts in front of a model
  may make it perform worse than the bare loop.
- **A user cell is the user's extension of the harness.** No user Reaction exists until the user
  writes one or adopts a repo entry (ADR 0104). Decision 5 already makes its *content* the user's
  responsibility (the standing obligation of ADR 0064 §6); this amendment makes the *slot* theirs
  too. A user cell may open on a new slot with no admission arm when that slot meets the
  structural bar:
  - **role-safe** under strict chat templates — no new message after a tool result, no system
    prompt;
  - **prefix-preserving** — the request up to the advised message is untouched;
  - **fenced** by a provenance-derived header nothing out-of-process can forge (decision 6);
  - **capped** at decision 7's limit, truncated with a marker;
  - **ephemeral** — stripped from the session record, dropped on resume;
  - **off under Bypass**;
  - its handlers under decision 8's posture — the workspace exec fence and secret redaction.
- **The session-start slot meets the bar.** A user `advise:` (argv or webhook) on
  `session-started` is admitted; the "pending admission" refusal is retired, and the bench arm of
  issue `session-advise-admission-arm` is no longer a prerequisite. Such an arm stays welcome
  evidence, never a gate.
- **Bypass is the user's reference.** With `bypass:` on, every user advise entry attaches nothing,
  so a user who suspects their advice hurts compares against the bare loop.
- **Unchanged:** the user shape (view) cell stays reserved — its open question is the ledger for
  edits, not admission; shape (work) stays engine-only; engine-origin advise (the context-fill and
  step-budget notices) still ships off until bench evidence turns it on.
