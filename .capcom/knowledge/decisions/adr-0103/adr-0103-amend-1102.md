---
title: "ADR 0103 amendment of 2026-11-02: the journal frame names the run's model and window"
---

Decided in the watch-run-projection run (docs/plans/2026-11-02 - 06 - watch-run-projection-plan.md). It qualifies decision 3. The journal's own `run_started` frame also carries `model` and `context_window` (`eventjson.JournalStarted`): the binding the run held when the journal was born. Both members are always written, and `""` and `0` mean unknown. Headless stdout frames still do not change, and a later rebind is not re-stamped into the frame.

A watch binds the two as it opens: the frame's window is the gauge's limit and the frame's model measures the watched run's sub-agent rows. A depth-0 usage line that names a window or a model then moves them. A journal without the members, from an older apogee, falls back to the record's model (`Meta.Model`) and to the watcher's own window, and a frame naming no window falls back to the watcher's own window the same way, each until the run's first usage reading names its own. The authority is: depth-0 usage line, then the journal frame, then the record's `Meta.Model` (model only), then the watcher's own binding.
