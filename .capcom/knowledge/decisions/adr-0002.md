> Note (2026-10-03): the Mechanism layer this ADR refers to was removed; ADR 0076 (Reactions) replaces it. Read the Mechanism-specific parts as historical.

> **Superseded in part by ADR 0076 (2026-09-07; noted 2026-09-24).** Its decision 1 deletes the Mechanism
> registry and catalogue, so the curated-catalogue half is history; the open-Tools half stands.

Embedders may register their own tools: the `Tool` interface and registry are public and open, and a
tool may attach a typed summary. That open-Tools half still holds; the curated Mechanism catalogue
of the other half was deleted by ADR 0076. Read `adr-0002-context`, `adr-0002-decision`, then
`adr-0002-amend-0820`, which corrects how many built-ins attach a summary.
