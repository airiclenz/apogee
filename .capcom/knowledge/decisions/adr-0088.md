---
title: Cancel settles and never rewinds finished work
---

Status: accepted
Supersedes: ADR 0013 §5(b) (a cancel rolls the whole parent Turn back and keeps no partial result), ADR 0007's "cancellation rolls the whole Turn back" consequence
Amends: ADR 0039 D4 (a cancelled pool no longer drops its group), ADR 0086 D3 (a cancel no longer restores the retained set), ADR 0011 C3 (the Step settles rather than rolls back), ADR 0075 D12 (a cancelled delegation is no longer one the parent Turn rolls back)

A cancel stops work and keeps everything that finished; it never rewinds. The Turn in flight is
settled, a delegation pool keeps its finished children, a workflow is stopped and kept, and finished
work on disk stays written. It supersedes ADR 0013 §5(b) and the cancel rollbacks of ADRs 0063, 0082
and 0086. Read `adr-0088-context`, `adr-0088-decision`, then `adr-0088-consequences`.
