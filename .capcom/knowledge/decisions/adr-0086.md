Status: accepted; D3's cancel row and the "cancel rolls that Turn back" consequence superseded by ADR 0088
Amends: ADR 0013 §5 and its 2026-09-18 amendment (a stopped child returns a partial result; retention outlives the Exchange), ADR 0022's 2026-09-18 addendum (retention is session state), ADR 0063 D4 and its "What stays out" consequence (per-child stop; prompting a finished child)

`sub_agent` stays blocking: a delegation the call named stays continuable for the session, seeded
from its trimmed history, retention is session state, and the human can stop one child by run id
while the Turn goes on. ADR 0088 superseded D3's cancel row and the cancel rollback; ADRs 0089 and
0094 narrowed the blocking rule. Read `adr-0086-context`, `adr-0086-decision`, `adr-0086-rejected`,
then `adr-0086-consequences`.
