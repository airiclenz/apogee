---
title: Confinement attaches to blast radius; Auto is `confine-to-workspace`-tunable — supersedes ADR 0004
---

Status: accepted
Supersedes: ADR 0004

Confinement attaches to blast radius: a call runs ungated only when OS confinement or path safety
bounds it, and Auto's global `confine-to-workspace` flag (default true) fences writes, network open.
Read `adr-0012-context`, `adr-0012-decision`, `adr-0012-rejected`, `adr-0012-consequences`, then
`adr-0012-amend-0721`, `adr-0012-amend-0725`, `adr-0012-amend-0726`, `adr-0012-amend-0906`,
`adr-0012-amend-0914`, `adr-0012-amend-0926`, `adr-0012-amend-0927` and `adr-0012-amend-1010`.
