title: "ADR 0040 amendment of 2026-11-05: `footer-accent` is the 30th key"
---

The 2026-08-15 amendment states the vocabulary as **29 keys**, and the consequences restate it as
"29 today": both counts stood until this one. `footer-accent` lands as the **30th key**, in exactly
the additive sense this record decides — a new key an absent scheme file silently inherits
(decision 3), so a user scheme written before it loads clean and the footer's model takes `dark`'s
value, pinned by `TestParseOmittedFooterAccentInheritsDark` in `internal/scheme/scheme_test.go`.
The vocabulary is **30 keys** from here, pinned by `wantRoleCount` in the same file; read the two
earlier counts as history.

- **`footer-accent`** is the footer's one accent among its muted facts: the model a session talks
  to, the fact a reader looks for first on the upstream row. It names a meaning the palette had no
  word for — a tint that lifts a fact out of the row without saying anything about it — so it is
  neither a `mode-*` rung nor `error`, and every shipped scheme is tested to keep it apart from
  those, from the footer git tones (`warning`, `success`) and from `muted`
  (`TestBuiltinSchemesKeepFooterAccentDistinct`). It sits between `muted-bright` and `user-text` in
  brightness, shipping `#c8c8f0` in `dark` and `#2d3a5a` in `light`, where "brighter" is darker.
  Its first consumer is the footer's model segment (plan
  `docs/plans/2026-11-05 - 02 - footer-rows-and-colour-plan.md`, item 8).

Nothing here reopens what this record decides: the key is additive, its values are scheme data
rather than decisions of this record, and the compatibility surface is intact.
