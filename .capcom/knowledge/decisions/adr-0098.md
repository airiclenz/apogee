The release process is committed to the repo: two host-agnostic skills do the judgment work and
`cmd/release` the deterministic, converging steps, gated on green CI and rendering manifests from
in-repo templates. Releases are cut on the owner's machine, a channel with pending setup is skipped,
and the owner's personal skills stand aside. Read `adr-0098-context`, `adr-0098-decision`,
`adr-0098-rejected`, `adr-0098-consequences`, then `adr-0098-amend-1105`.
